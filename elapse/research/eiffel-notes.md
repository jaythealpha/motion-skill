# Eiffel Tower construction study — 2026-11-01

## Evidence and chosen scope
- [SETE construction history](https://www.toureiffel.paris/en/the-monument/history): official milestone schedule and general construction methods. Only facts are used; all application prose and geometry are independently authored.
- [SETE key figures](https://www.toureiffel.paris/en/the-monument/key-figures), with the [metric-language table](https://www.toureiffel.paris/de/monument/zahlen): reference square width, pillar width and rounded platform levels. The official total historical height is distinct from the300m main-structure design used here. This mesh does not reproduce the full flagpole/top fittings or modern antennas.
- [SETE historical colour](https://www.toureiffel.paris/en/news/history-and-culture/133-years-and-1083-feet): red-brown period finish. The exact RGB/material response is estimated.
- Initial date is a January1887 presentation boundary, not evidence of activity on January1. The English official history lists26January; a City of Paris project PDF search index describes a28January construction boundary. Direct PDF access was too large for the browser fetch, so its full contents were not reviewed. The demo uses month labels rather than asserting a precise disputed opening day. Last chapter holds the completion day for inspection.

## Estimated presentation
The `PROFILE` station widths between the nominal base and main-structure tip, individual member sections, joints, platform rings, pavilions, arches, upper cabin/roof, crane geometry and their coordinates are diagrammatic estimates. They are not extracted from a surveyed mesh or original plate dimensions. Decorative members and temporary works have illustrative reveal intervals; actual daily erection order is not reconstructed. The member count is a renderer diagnostic, not the historic inventory. Nominal Gate1 checks use member-node bounds; beam thickness and omitted fittings are separate approximations.

Four lower lattice legs transition to an upper lattice shaft. First/second platforms and crown appear as separate stages. Temporary wooden supports and cranes explain construction and disappear at their approximate stages; no people are depicted. Subgrade blocks are deliberately exposed above a cutaway ground plane for legibility; this is not a surveyed excavation plan. Current glass-floor treatments, modern restaurants, antenna extensions, city skyline and modern light shows are absent.

Height interpolates between reference anchors; the first-level beam connection and floor-completion dates remain distinct. The displayed height ratio is not percent of labour, material or engineering completion. Platform/roof appearance and scaffold removal are estimated. Motion is deterministic in historical time, so dragging backward restores the same state. Reduced motion prevents automatic query autoplay; explicit playback remains user-controlled.

The building branch has its own dimension and chronology checks, renderer and gauges. It shares source marking, playback, fixed slots and exploration with Elapse; ship flooding/wreck checks do not certify it. Unsupported building templates are rejected. No external photos, scans, video frames, meshes or fonts were downloaded or embedded. No NIST/9·11 or Notre-Dame geometry has been implemented in this pass.
