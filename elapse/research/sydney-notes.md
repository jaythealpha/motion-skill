# Sydney Opera House: sources, people, facts and estimated context

Checked2026-11-04. The initial task was to exercise the default new-topic workflow from source research through a public demo. Source records were retrieved with the web tools before the evidence bundle was written; their access dates are not independent corroboration.

## Retrieved sources

- [Sydney Opera House, The Creators Project](https://www.sydneyoperahouse.com/our-story/creators-project): full text.8 November2023 plaque unveiling; Utzon, Arup, Hall and collective creativity. Named roles and Gadigal/Tubowgule acknowledgement. Institutional account, not complete labor history.
- [The spherical solution](https://www.sydneyoperahouse.com/our-story/the-spherical-solution): full text.1958–1962 iterations; architects/engineers and repeatable geometry. Does not justify a literal orange-peeling discovery reenactment. The procedural sail control points are independently estimated, not the official spherical geometry.
- [Jørn Utzon AC](https://www.sydneyoperahouse.com/our-story/jorn-utzon): full text.29 January1957 selection. The page's entry-count wording differs from the NSW archive search excerpt; no competition-entry total was adopted.
- [NFSA, Landmark: construction of the Sydney Opera House](https://www.nfsa.gov.au/collection/item/landmark-construction-sydney-opera-house): full collection notes attributed to Beth Taylor. Construction1 March1959, opening20 October1973; about10,000 construction participants.1972 is film metadata, not opening. The film, stills and audio were not downloaded or embedded; the notes do not establish a complete deduplicated payroll or simultaneous headcount.
- [Opera House, Renewing an icon](https://stories.sydneyoperahouse.com/renewing-an-icon/): full text. Concert Hall February2020–July2022;18 reflectors,400mm relative stage lowering, lift/passageway. Other sections contain historical plans, which were not treated as today's completed state.
- [ARM, Celebrating a Milestone](https://armarchitecture.com.au/news/celebrating-a-milestone-sydney-opera-house-reopens/): full project participant account, published12 August2022, describing July reopening; Andrew Hayne, ARM and Müller-BBM;12+4+2 reflector functions and40cm lowering. Participant account rather than independent acoustic measurement.

## Values and chronology

Five records contain seven contributor entries and three shared numeric facts: approximately10,000 participants over1959–1973;18 Concert Hall reflectors in2022;0.4m lowering relative to the former stage. The last is not sea-level elevation. Counts refer to different scopes and are not combined. Preserve exact dates for competition/start/opening/plaques, and month precision for July2022 reopening. Neither article publication nor reopening means all people worked that day or conservation ended.

The source scene is independent of `remembrance`; `build-topic.mjs` attaches the bundle. The compiler checks supplied evidence coverage and languages; it does not do web research or authenticate the historical claims. The tested agent run performed that research. A lack of evidence must be visibly documented, never filled with invented figures.

## Illustration scope

Separate podium terraces and monumental stair, nine estimated paired/clustered sail groups, approximate ribs/seams/glazing, and a deliberately exposed Concert Hall with18 schematic magenta reflectors. The nine groups are a modeling choice, not an official shell count. Shape, paving, intermediate reveal, hall layout, seat pattern, reflector positions and the colored access path are estimates, not surveyed plans, actual installation routes or acoustic/accessibility certification. The shell surface is an independent smooth interpretation; it is not a verified common-sphere CAD reconstruction.

No people are rendered. Exterior shells remain in the2022 view; renewal is seen in the clearly described hall/renewal cutaway, not by rebuilding the historical exterior. Available stops are date-bounded. Major chapter boundaries between sourced events are presentation intervals;1963 and1967 geometry-reveal anchors are estimates. The endpoint is8 November2023 recognition, not a claim about today's whole building.

The preconstruction context includes a schematic grid/quay/water surface, not a surveyed1957 site or a traced competition plan. Glazing is hidden until fit-out; it is never shown as an already built structure at the competition stage.
