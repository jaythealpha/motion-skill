Checked2026-11-04. The initial task was to exercise the default new-topic workflow from source research through a public demo. Source records were retrieved with the web tools before the evidence bundle was written; their access dates are not independent corroboration.

## Architectural detail revision — 2026-11-04

Consulted Australian Government World Heritage description (https://www.dcceew.gov.au/parks-heritage/heritage/places/world/sydney-opera-house): three interlocking roof groups; two slightly splayed, generally north–south halls; stages towards the city/south; precast ribs between pedestals and ridge beams; off-white ceramic tiles and earth-toned granite podium. Corrected the original model's north-stage arrangement. New paired patches lie on equal-radius spheres with overlapping sails, roof underside and edge thickness, rib courses and cladding bands. The working radius75m is an explicit modelling assumption, not an attributed measurement: the Arup web account's75m diameter conflicts with radius descriptions elsewhere. Exact shell cuts, coordinates and ribs are independently authored estimates, not a digitised plan or engineering analysis.

Consulted the operator's Concert Hall Technical and Production Information, issue25 January2024 (https://www.sydneyoperahouse.com/sites/default/files/collaborodam_assets/SOHVenueTechnicalSpecifications_ConcertHall202401.pdf), pp3,7–8,13–14: brushbox finishes, birch/magenta seats, normal centreline stage depth12.30m, front14.50m, auditorium offset0.90m; eighteen overhead reflectors with configurable trim8.5–12m;22 wall reflectors,11 per side. This later specification informs the2022-renewed explanatory interior, not a new2024 event. The published capacities differ by configuration/source; no illustrated seat count is presented as venue capacity. Seats, balconies, pipes, mullions, acoustic element shapes and coordinates remain estimates. No acoustic or accessibility certification.

Utzon Design Principles, May2002 (https://www.sydneyoperahouse.com/sites/default/files/collaborodam_assets/utzon-design-principles.pdf) supplied architectural descriptions, not copied plans.
