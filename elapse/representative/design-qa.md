# Titanic separation study — 2026-10-31

Status: passed (representative-scene browser and framing checks).
Scope: a single representative scene, not a release of the whole timeline.

## Implemented
- Side-on close framing, gradual pullback as bow/stern separate.
- Procedural moving water, impact rings, foam and deterministic spray particles.
- 30-second scene with a slower passage around separation, plus speed selector.
- Scrubbing and three scene jumps; Korean labels and focus-visible styles.
- Reduced-motion preference disables requested autoplay.
- Offline HTML, regenerated by `node elapse/scripts/build-break-scene.mjs`.
