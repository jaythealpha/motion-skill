## Interpretation limits

Read `../research/wtc-notes.md` for primary links and record resolution. Official sources supply event times and reopening milestones. Aircraft silhouettes/short approach segments, impact flashes, burning bands, smoke, dust, disappearing envelopes, debris and all intermediate construction are illustrative. Two-minute collapse-animation windows are not measured durations. This is **not NIST simulation, a validated physical collapse, structural/fire guidance, actual flight-path reconstruction or a playable attack scenario**.

Local site coordinates, neighbouring low-rise blocks, glazing, ornament, footprint framing, trees and most new-building dimensions are schematic. No plan scan was traced or surveyed geometry certified. Only selected structures are included, ending at the11June2018 milestone; it does not depict the entire current campus or completed redevelopment, and no completed2 WTC is invented. Times are New York local wall-clock. No people, victims, human remains or fabricated name inscriptions are drawn. Intentional close-ups crop neighbouring structures. Canvas-only exports omit SVG annotation overlays, an existing limitation.
